# Source notes and canon reconciliation

Abby's initial role and tone came from the working series canon in `Definitely-Ai/Cartoon`: a Westie bartender at The Swinging Door, wise, helpful, respectful, faintly patient, usually working behind the bar, and used sparingly so her lines land hard.

This repository records the later approved design pass and therefore supersedes older working visual defaults wherever they conflict.

## Preserved from the original series canon

- Adult anthropomorphic West Highland White Terrier.
- Bartender at The Swinging Door.
- Wise, helpful, observant, and never cruel.
- Speaks less than the regulars; short lines close the loop.
- Usually appears while doing bar work.
- Black-and-white American magazine-cartoon language.

## Superseded working defaults

- Abby now **owns** The Swinging Door in addition to tending bar.
- Her eyes are human-style expressive eyes integrated into a canine face, not simple dots.
- A broad, welcoming smile is allowed and is her default public expression.
- Her full body may be shown for owner, entrance, opening, closing, or action beats.
- Her final outfit, proportions, jewelry, and no-tail design are defined in the Abby bible.
- Her signature neckpiece is a close-fitting pearl strand with a centered oval gemstone, replacing the earlier plain dark collar.

These decisions are intentional canon changes, not model-sheet accidents.
