# Abby — visual specification

## Shape language

Abby's silhouette combines a compact Westie head, upright ears, a softly curved feminine torso, long clean leg lines, and practical heels. Curves communicate warmth and confidence; small angular accents in brows, collar points, skirt edge, and heels communicate intelligence and authority.

At thumbnail size, recognition should come from:

1. triangular Westie ears and textured white head;
2. human-style eyes and small black canine nose;
3. pearl collar with centered oval gemstone;
4. fitted blouse and short dark apron-skirt;
5. slim bare legs and black heels;
6. no-tail rear silhouette.

## Head construction

- Head is slightly wider through the cheek fur than through the brow.
- Ears sit high and remain upright; do not flop them.
- Muzzle projects clearly from the face and ends in a small solid black nose.
- The mouth follows the muzzle, never a human lip shape.
- Chin fur forms a soft point but not a beard.
- Cheek fur radiates outward in short layered clumps; forehead fur rises between the ears.
- Keep the face feminine through eye design, lashes, brow control, and clean cheek shape—not by erasing the muzzle.

## Eye construction

The eyes are human-style in their expressive mechanics, but belong naturally in a canine skull.

- Medium-sized almond-to-rounded eye opening.
- Visible white sclera around a medium-gray iris.
- Iris contains subtle radial graphite texture.
- Round black pupil with two small clean catchlights.
- Clear upper lid, lighter lower lid, refined upper lashes.
- Brow-fur acts like eyebrows and carries much of the expression.
- Both eyes share the same focal target unless a deliberate comic beat says otherwise.
- In profile, show one complete eye set into the side plane; do not paste a frontal eye onto profile anatomy.

Avoid giant irises, empty gray discs, asymmetrical pupil sizes, crossed gaze, overly wet photoreal highlights, or human eyelid skin.

## Body proportions

- Adult feminine hourglass build.
- Fuller bust, fitted but tasteful blouse.
- Narrow waist at apron top.
- Slim hips with a clean skirt silhouette.
- Legs longer than the compact torso suggests, slim and shapely rather than muscular.
- A natural thigh gap is visible in neutral front and back stances.
- Knees and calves are lightly defined with contour and minimal wash.
- Feet remain inside closed-toe heels; never canine hind paws.

## Hands

- Anthropomorphic five-digit hands.
- Fingers are slim, practical, and readable.
- Grip glasses at structurally plausible points.
- When polishing, one hand supports the vessel and the other controls the towel.
- Avoid duplicated fingers, fused fingertips, ambiguous thumbs, or floating props.

## Fur and surface texture

- Facial and head fur: highest detail, short layered ink strokes following growth direction.
- Neck and wrist fur: lighter transitional texture.
- Exposed legs: smooth and hairless, using clean contour and a light graphite value only.
- Blouse: paper white with sparse seam and fold lines.
- Skirt/apron: one mid-gray wash with a subtle restrained geometric texture.
- Pearls: small round paper-white beads, each separated by a fine dark contour.
- Gemstone: oval, faceted, mid-gray core, dark bezel, one restrained highlight.

## Wardrobe construction

### Blouse

Light fitted collared blouse, darts and folds following the torso. Sleeves end at the forearms in tidy rolled cuffs. The top button remains open; the neckline reveals only a modest scalloped lace inset. The blouse is attractive but work-capable.

### Skirt/apron

Very short, dark, fitted, and high-waisted. The hem is straight to subtly curved and remains practical. The rear bow is centered and clearly tied. There is no tail slit, opening, tuft, or bulge.

### Jewelry and work details

- Pearl collar sits close to the neck as one continuous strand.
- Oval gemstone hangs exactly at center front.
- Bracelet is delicate and subordinate to the collar.
- Folded bar towel is clean, narrow, and draped over one shoulder.
- Heels are black, closed-toe, mid-height, and stable enough for bar work.

## Posture

- Default stance: weight mostly on one leg, chest open, shoulders relaxed, head attentive.
- Owner stance: both hands on bar or one hand on hip; stable and unhurried.
- Listening stance: slight forward lean, ear and gaze directed toward speaker.
- Witty aside: small raised finger or palm-up gesture, not theatrical gesticulation.
- Firm authority: squared shoulders, level chin, compressed lids, quiet hand signal.

## Rendering law

- Black ink and graphite/ink wash only on warm off-white paper.
- Confident variable-weight contour.
- Fine fur strokes; clean clothing contours; sparse shadow wash.
- Mid-century American magazine-cartoon sensibility.
- No color, photorealism, glossy digital airbrush, 3D, anime, or vector-flat look.
- Model sheets may use clear hand-lettered headings and labels; final story art should contain only story-required text.
