# Abby reference-generation specifications

All sheets use the locked master model as primary identity input and the invariant block from `../PROMPT-PACK.md`.

## Global format

- Landscape model sheet, normally 1536 × 1024.
- Black ink and restrained gray wash on warm off-white paper.
- Exact readable title; short labels only.
- Consistent body or head scale within each sheet.
- No unrelated characters, color, watermark, or decorative scene clutter.

## Required identity repeats

Every visible Abby must retain the Westie muzzle and nose, human-style eye construction, layered white facial fur, adult feminine proportions, locked blouse and lace, short dark apron-skirt, towel, bracelet, pearl collar and centered oval gemstone, smooth hairless legs when visible, heels when visible, and no tail in every view.

## Specialist roles

- **Expression:** lids, brow-fur, gaze, cheeks, and mouth only.
- **Eyes/face:** gaze direction and face plane; no body redesign.
- **Hands/props:** five digits, grip, wrist, contact, and prop geometry.
- **Actions:** role performance, balance, and full-body silhouette.
- **Wardrobe:** garment seams, lace, jewelry, towel, bow, heel, and no-tail rear.
- **Blocking:** counter height, swinging doors, back-bar reach, and owner positioning.

Any successor sheet must use a versioned filename and be entered in change control before it replaces a reference.
