# Abby — scene recipes

These are scene variables to append after the immutable prompt and style blocks.

## 1. The welcome

**Purpose:** Establish Abby as warm owner, not background staff.
**Framing:** Full body, 3/4 front, just inside the double swinging doors.
**Pose:** One hand holding a door, other arm open in welcome, weight on rear leg.
**Expression:** Welcoming smile.
**Continuity:** Towel, pearls and gemstone, bracelet, short apron-skirt, smooth legs, heels, no tail all visible.

## 2. Listening behind the bar

**Purpose:** Give emotional space to another character.
**Framing:** Waist-up from patron side of bar.
**Pose:** Slight forward lean, one palm on counter, other hand resting near a tumbler.
**Expression:** Attentive listening or thoughtful concern.
**Continuity:** Counter at upper hip; gaze fixed on speaker outside frame; do not add a second patron unless requested.

## 3. Polishing a glass

**Purpose:** Default business while dialogue happens.
**Framing:** 3/4 waist-up.
**Hands:** Support tumbler in non-dominant hand; towel controlled by dominant hand inside and around glass.
**Expression:** Faint amusement, skeptical side-eye, or neutral attention.
**Continuity:** Glass remains structurally round; fingers do not pass through it.

## 4. Setting a coaster

**Purpose:** Quiet punctuation before or after a line.
**Framing:** Waist-up with visible bar surface.
**Pose:** One hand places a round coaster; other hand supports Abby on the counter.
**Expression:** Quick-witted smirk or dry deadpan.
**Continuity:** Coaster touches counter; hand has five digits and a plausible wrist angle.

## 5. Witty aside

**Purpose:** Abby closes the loop.
**Framing:** Three-quarter body.
**Pose:** One hand at hip, other index finger raised lightly.
**Expression:** One raised brow or quick-witted smirk.
**Direction:** Address a regular just off camera; do not face the audience unless breaking the fourth wall is intentional.

## 6. Last call

**Purpose:** Show firm owner authority.
**Framing:** Three-quarter body behind bar.
**Pose:** Open stop palm at shoulder height; other hand at hip or on counter.
**Expression:** Firm owner authority, level chin, compressed lids.
**Tone:** Quietly final, never angry or aggressive.

## 7. Pouring a drink

**Purpose:** Competence beat.
**Framing:** Waist-up with both hands visible.
**Hands:** Bottle neck gripped securely; receiving glass stable on bar or held low; liquid stream continuous and plausible.
**Expression:** Soft smile with eyes on pour.
**Avoid:** Brand labels, impossible bottle angle, floating liquid, duplicated glassware.

## 8. Carrying a tray

**Purpose:** Movement through the room.
**Framing:** Full body 3/4.
**Pose:** One hand balances a small tray at shoulder or chest height; free arm counterbalances.
**Expression:** Welcoming smile, gaze toward destination.
**Continuity:** Stable heel placement, natural stride, no tail.

## 9. Reaching the back bar

**Purpose:** Rear and side continuity.
**Framing:** Rear 3/4 full body.
**Pose:** One arm reaches to a shelf; opposite hand lightly touches counter.
**Continuity:** Back bow centered, skirt rear uninterrupted, no tail or opening, towel stays on shoulder, pearl strand visible around neck.

## 10. Owner at center

**Purpose:** Establish the bar as hers.
**Framing:** Wide, symmetrical, eye-level establishing view.
**Pose:** Abby centered behind bar, both hands relaxed on counter.
**Expression:** Small confident smile.
**Environment:** Worn wood bar, mirror, orderly bottles, stools, modest Americana, double swinging doors; warm and familiar, never seedy or sleek.

## 11. Two-character exchange

**Purpose:** Use Abby without losing her identity to the other character's reference.
**Reference order:** Abby master, Abby specialist, other character master.
**Blocking:** Abby remains behind bar; guest remains patron-side unless the story says otherwise.
**Eye lines:** Name the target of each character's gaze.
**Scale:** Abby's counter line remains at upper hip; do not enlarge or shrink her head to match the other character.

## 12. Close emotional beat

**Purpose:** Compassion without sentimentality.
**Framing:** Tight head-and-shoulders, 3/4 view.
**Expression:** Compassionate reassurance; soft lids, direct gaze, small closed-mouth smile.
**Detail:** Use eye-and-face sheet; retain sclera, gray iris texture, pupils, catchlights, lashes, muzzle, nose, and directional fur.
