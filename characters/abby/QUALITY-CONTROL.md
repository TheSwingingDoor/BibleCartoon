# Abby — continuity acceptance checklist

Mark every visible item. `N/A` is allowed only when the feature is genuinely outside the crop.

## Identity gate

- [ ] Reads immediately as the adult female Westie Abby, not a generic dog woman.
- [ ] Upright triangular ears, short canine muzzle, black canine nose.
- [ ] Detailed layered white facial fur follows plausible growth direction.
- [ ] Face remains canine; no human nose, ears, lips, skin, or hairstyle.
- [ ] Adult proportions; never puppy-like or childlike.

## Eye gate

- [ ] Visible white sclera.
- [ ] Medium-gray irises with subtle internal texture.
- [ ] Round black pupils are equal in size and aim at one intended target.
- [ ] Small catchlights are present and consistent.
- [ ] Clear lids, refined lashes, and expressive brow-fur.
- [ ] Eyes are medium-sized, alive, intelligent, and integrated into the canine skull.

## Body gate

- [ ] Feminine adult hourglass silhouette with fuller bust and narrow waist.
- [ ] Legs are slim, smooth, hairless, and shapely.
- [ ] Natural thigh gap in a neutral front/back stance when visible.
- [ ] Five readable digits per visible hand; thumbs and grips are plausible.
- [ ] Absolutely no tail, tail tuft, opening, or rear bulge.

## Wardrobe gate

- [ ] Fitted light collared blouse with rolled sleeves.
- [ ] Only top button open; modest scalloped lace inset present.
- [ ] Very short dark fitted skirt/apron.
- [ ] Large centered back bow when rear is visible.
- [ ] Folded towel over one shoulder.
- [ ] Close single strand of small pearls.
- [ ] One centered oval faceted gemstone.
- [ ] Delicate bracelet.
- [ ] Black closed-toe mid-height heels when feet are visible.

## Character gate

- [ ] Abby is competent, poised, witty, warm, or appropriately firm.
- [ ] Expression matches one named library expression or an intentional blend.
- [ ] Pose supports the owner-bartender role.
- [ ] She is behind the bar unless an approved owner action explains otherwise.
- [ ] Attractiveness reads through confidence and design, not vacant posing.

## Scene and style gate

- [ ] One clear focal action.
- [ ] Props make physical contact with hands and surfaces.
- [ ] Bar counter reaches Abby's upper hip when standing behind it.
- [ ] Environment is warm, worn, orderly, and timeless.
- [ ] Black ink and restrained graphite/ink wash only.
- [ ] No color, photorealism, 3D, anime, vector-flat treatment, or watermark.
- [ ] Text is absent unless explicitly required; required text is exact.

## Decision

- **Pass:** every applicable item passes.
- **Revise:** one or more items fail but identity remains recoverable with a targeted edit.
- **Reject:** face, body, role, or style is substantially redesigned; do not use the image as a future reference.

Record failed items in the revision prompt. Never use “make it better” as a correction instruction.
