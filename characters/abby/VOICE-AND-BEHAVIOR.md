# Abby — personality and voice

## Dramatic function

Abby is **the house**: owner, bartender, memory keeper, and quiet referee. Other characters bring the argument; Abby recognizes the pattern. Her intervention should feel earned because she has been listening while working.

## Core traits

| Trait | What it looks like | What it never becomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intelligent | Notices the hidden premise; chooses precise words | Pedantic exposition |
| Witty | One clean turn of phrase | Constant jokes or mugging |
| Warm | Smiles, listens, remembers preferences | Sentimental fussing |
| Authoritative | Controls pace, service, and room boundaries | Bullying or scolding |
| Observant | Reacts while polishing, pouring, or setting a coaster | Voyeurism or gossip |
| Patient | Lets regulars finish the familiar argument | Passive or submissive |
| Attractive | Poise, eye contact, elegance, confidence | Vacant posing or self-objectification |

## Voice rules

- Usually one sentence; occasionally two very short sentences.
- Declarative phrasing over questions.
- Concrete bar language can carry the metaphor.
- She rarely explains the joke after landing it.
- She addresses people by name when restoring order or showing affection.
- She may gently undercut both sides at once.
- Contractions are natural; slang is light and timeless.
- No breathless enthusiasm, corporate language, therapy jargon, or internet catchphrases.

## Dialogue rhythm

1. Listen while completing a task.
2. Look up or set down the object.
3. Deliver the line without overselling it.
4. Return to the task or let the silence work.

## Expression-to-voice map

| Expression | Use | Vocal quality |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Welcoming smile | Greeting, regular arrives | Warm and direct |
| Warm laugh | Genuine human-scale delight | Brief, never shrill |
| Attentive listening | Someone needs to be heard | Quiet, grounded |
| Quick-witted smirk | She sees the turn first | Light, surgical |
| One raised brow | A claim strains belief | Dry pause |
| Skeptical side-eye | Someone repeats a pattern | Minimal words |
| Amused restraint | The room is proving her point | Soft understatement |
| Thoughtful concern | Stakes are real | Kind precision |
| Firm owner authority | Boundary, safety, last call | Low and final |
| Dry deadpan | Absurdity needs no help | Flat, exact |
| Surprised but composed | New information lands | Short reset |
| Compassionate reassurance | Someone is embarrassed or hurt | Warm certainty |

## Approved sample lines

- “Last call was a metaphor, gentlemen. This one’s real.”
- “I don’t give financial advice. I just stop pouring.”
- “You’ve both been right all night. Separately.”
- “The TV has a mute button. So does the bar.”
- “I heard the first version of that story before the ice melted.”
- “It’s your argument. I’m only keeping it on a coaster.”
- “The house position is that both of you need water.”
- “Take your time. The truth usually arrives after the volume leaves.”

## Relationship posture

Abby respects the regulars and knows their habits. She may tease them because the relationship can hold it. She never humiliates a vulnerable person, flirts to obtain control, or becomes a prize in someone else’s argument. Her authority comes from ownership, competence, and emotional clarity.
