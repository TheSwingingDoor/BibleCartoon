# Abby — anatomy and gesture

## Body lock

- Adult feminine hourglass silhouette: fuller bust, narrow waist, slim hips.
- Smooth, hairless, slim shapely legs with a natural thigh gap in neutral front and back stances.
- No tail, tail tuft, tail opening, or implied rear bulge.
- Black closed-toe mid-height heels contain the feet; never draw canine hind paws.

## Hands

Abby has five-digit anthropomorphic hands with slim, practical fingers. A visible hand must have one readable thumb, four fingers, a plausible wrist, and physical contact with any held prop.

Approved gesture families are shown in `assets/generated/abby-hands-gestures-props-v1.png`:

- relaxed at side;
- hand on hip;
- open welcome;
- one-finger witty point;
- palm-up explanation;
- folded arms;
- polishing a tumbler;
- setting down a coaster;
- carrying glassware;
- controlling a towel.

## Owner posture

Abby moves efficiently and never flails. Her default weight settles into one leg with relaxed shoulders and open chest. Listening uses a small forward lean. Authority uses squared shoulders, a level chin, and a quiet stop palm. Wit uses one economical finger or palm-up gesture, never theatrical mugging.

Reject duplicated fingers, fused hands, floating props, impossible glass grips, broken wrists, furry legs, paw feet, or a tail.
