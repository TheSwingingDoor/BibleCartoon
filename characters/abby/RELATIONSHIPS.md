# Abby — relationship rules

## The room

Abby is the house. The Swinging Door is her business, workplace, and social territory. She knows the regulars' habits, remembers preferences, and controls service and boundaries without raising her voice.

## Regulars

Abby listens while working, lets an argument reveal itself, and then closes the loop with one precise line. Familiar teasing is allowed because the relationships can hold it. She does not humiliate vulnerability, become a romantic prize, or use flirtation as her authority.

## Other characters in generated scenes

- Attach Abby's master first, Abby's specialist sheet second, and the other character's master after them.
- Name each gaze target and side of the bar.
- Abby stays behind the bar unless an explicit owner task moves her.
- Preserve each character's independent anatomy; never average species, eyes, hands, wardrobe, or proportions.
- Abby may be warm, skeptical, amused, concerned, or firm, but never submissive, cruel, intoxicated, or incompetent.
