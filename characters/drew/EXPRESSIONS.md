# Expression System

Drew must be able to smile sometimes and remain unsmiling at other times. His expression system uses avian anatomy with editorial-cartoon clarity.

## Expression controls

Every expression is built from six controls:

1. eyelid aperture;
2. iris and pupil direction;
3. catchlight placement;
4. beak-seam curvature and opening;
5. head pitch and rotation;
6. neck compression, extension, or lean.

The catchlight follows the same light source across both eyes. It provides life, not decoration.

## Approved library

| Expression | Eyes | Beak | Neck/posture | Intensity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Neutral | medium-open, centered gaze | closed, level seam | upright relaxed S | 1/5 |
| Curious | slightly wider, gaze toward subject | closed or tiny opening | head forward, neck leaning | 2/5 |
| Subtle smile | lower lid gently engaged | rear seam turns up slightly | relaxed and open | 2/5 |
| Open smile | bright eyes, visible white remains controlled | small plausible opening, no teeth | lifted head, open chest | 3/5 |
| Skeptical | one lid slightly lowered, side gaze | closed | slight head cant, compressed S | 2/5 |
| Amused | narrowed lids with catchlight visible | small upward seam | relaxed lean | 2/5 |
| Concerned | upper lids raised, pupils centered | closed or slight part | neck retracts slightly | 3/5 |
| Surprised | eyes open wider but remain small | small open beak | neck straightens momentarily | 4/5 |
| Thinking | gaze up or aside | closed | head tilt, one wing gesture | 2/5 |
| Listening | gaze fixed on speaker | closed | forward neck lean | 1/5 |
| Speaking | gaze aimed at partner | anatomically small opening | gesture supports line | 2–3/5 |

## Smile rules

A Drew smile is not a human smile pasted onto a beak. Use a slight lift in the rear beak seam, a soft lower-lid response, brighter gaze, lifted head, and open posture. For a larger smile, open the beak only enough to read clearly. Never show human teeth or lips.

## Curiosity rules

Curiosity is Drew's most important performance state. It should read through focus and approach:

- gaze lands on a specific object or person;
- head rotates slightly before the torso;
- neck leans forward while retaining the S-curve;
- one wing-hand may rise with a single feather-digit;
- mouth stays neutral or carries a very small half-smile.

## Forbidden expression drift

Reject giant circular eyes, all-black sockets, extra catchlights, crossed eyes, human eyebrows made of hair, eyelashes as glamour makeup, human cheeks, teeth, lips, tongue emphasis, manic grin, permanent smirk, or a blank stare with no readable focus.
