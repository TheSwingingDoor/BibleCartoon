# Reference Generation Specifications

These specifications record the intended contents of each supporting sheet. To regenerate one, attach the locked master first, paste the invariant, style, and negative blocks from `../prompts.json`, and then add the matching variable block below.

Every regenerated candidate receives a new versioned filename and begins with status `review`.

## `drew-expression-library-v1`

Wide 3×4 grid of head-and-neck studies at consistent scale. Exact states: neutral, curious, subtle smile, open smile, skeptical, amused, concerned, surprised, thinking, listening, speaking, quietly pleased. The curious state is primary. Eye size, skull, beak, and neck cannot change between expressions.

## `drew-wing-hand-anatomy-v1`

Eight studies: relaxed, question, explain, point, martini grip, bar lean, writing, shrug. Include a construction diagram for shoulder coverts, elbow feather direction, forewing feathers, one thumb-feather, and two finger-feathers. Nail tips remain tiny and pale. Martini uses exactly three olives.

## `drew-pose-library-v1`

Eight full-body poses at equal scale: neutral stand, curious lean, listening, explaining, walking, seated, bar lean, subtle smile. Keep the entire figure visible and use only minimal stool or bar geometry.

## `drew-wardrobe-fit-v1`

Seven equal full-body studies: base model, bar jacket, business suit, golf, fishing, travel coat, formal. Clothing is a removable scene layer over unchanged anatomy. Bow tie remains visible; wing-hands remain feathered.

## `drew-scene-continuity-v1`

Four equal vignettes: The Swinging Door, golf course, fishing, travel. The same Drew appears in each environment at comparable scale. Identity stays fixed while clothing, action, prop, and background change.

## `drew-proportion-style-v1`

Four sections: proportion grid, front/three-quarter/profile silhouettes, identity close-ups, and line/wash swatches. The eye close-up must remain avian, with controlled visible white and no human eyebrow or glamour lashes.
