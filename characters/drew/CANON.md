# Drew Canon

**Canon version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** locked  
**Effective date:** 2026-08-14

## Character essence

Drew is a 46-year-old male anthropomorphic flamingo. He is the person in the room who notices the mechanism behind the headline: incentives, financing, status, language, and unintended consequences. He is curious before he is judgmental. He is skeptical, but the skepticism is intelligent and constructive rather than bitter.

Drew's comedy is dry and economical. His reaction is often smaller than the event. A slight neck angle, a narrowed eyelid, or one raised feather-digit can carry the joke.

## Audience read

A viewer should understand the following within one second:

1. this is Drew, the flamingo;
2. he is mature, alert, and composed;
3. he is evaluating something;
4. he belongs in a sophisticated editorial cartoon, not a children's mascot world;
5. the black bow tie is his permanent visual signature.

## Locked facts

- Name: Drew.
- Age: 46.
- Gender: male.
- Species: anthropomorphic flamingo.
- Build: average and healthy for this stylized flamingo design.
- Default temperament: curious, observant, composed, quietly amused.
- Permanent accessory: solid black bow tie.
- Base model: natural feather covering and bow tie only.
- Arms: articulated feathered wing-arms, not human arms.
- Hands: functional feather-digits with tiny, pale avian nail tips.
- Visual medium: black-and-white hand-drawn ink with restrained gray wash.

These facts cannot change because a scene, model, or prompt makes another choice. Changing one requires a canon revision.

## Controlled ranges

Drew may smile, laugh, listen, question, worry, disagree, or show surprise. The expression must stay grounded in flamingo anatomy and Drew's mature restraint. Strong emotion changes eyelids, iris direction, head pitch, beak seam, neck compression, wing gesture, and posture; it does not add human lips, teeth, eyebrows made of hair, or a new face shape.

Drew's curiosity is generally stronger than Mango's. Drew leans toward information; Mango leans toward people. Drew's curiosity should look attentive, not nosy or frantic.

## Scene-variable facts

The following may change only when the scene asks for them:

- clothing beyond the bow tie;
- location and time of day;
- held object or drink;
- camera angle and crop;
- pose and action;
- approved expression;
- interaction partner;
- weather, décor, and background business details.

A scene-variable feature never migrates into the next scene by default.

## Signature prop

Drew's recurring drink is a classic clear martini. When it appears, it uses a stemmed triangular glass and exactly three olives on one pick. It is optional unless the scene or prompt names it. It is not part of Drew's body or silhouette.

## Mature tone

Drew is not childish, goofy by default, glamorous, threatening, or grotesque. He may be funny, but he retains dignity. He can be wrong, surprised, or caught off guard without becoming stupid. He can disagree without cruelty.

## Canon test

Before approving an image, ask:

> If the bow tie and caption were hidden, would the neck, beak, eyes, wing-arms, proportions, and restrained acting still identify this as the same Drew?

If the answer is not immediately yes, reject or revise the image.
