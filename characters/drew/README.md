# Drew — Canon 1.0.0

Drew is a 46-year-old male anthropomorphic flamingo and the arch observer of **The Swinging Door**. He is elegant without being delicate, skeptical without being cynical, and more naturally curious than Mango. His humor comes from precise observation, restrained reactions, and the sense that he has already followed the money one step farther than everyone else.

## Locked visual signature

- long, slim, strongly curved flamingo neck;
- compact flamingo head and correctly downturned two-tone beak;
- expressive avian eyes with visible white, iris, pupil, and a small catchlight;
- average healthy flamingo build;
- articulated feathered wing-arms;
- three feather-digits per wing-hand with tiny understated avian nail tips;
- long legs and webbed flamingo feet;
- solid black bow tie, always present;
- detailed hand-drawn black ink and restrained gray wash on warm off-white paper.
