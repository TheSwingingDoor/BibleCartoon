# Relationships and Ensemble Rules

## Mango

Mango is the earnest heart of the duo; Drew is the arch observer. Drew tends to follow systems and information, while Mango tends to respond to people and immediate experience. Drew may be more curious about why something happened; Mango may be more concerned with what it means to someone.

Their contrast must remain affectionate. Drew does not belittle Mango. Mango does not exist only to make Drew look smarter. Either character may carry the insight or the emotional truth.

## Future characters

Two additional character bibles are expected. Until those character records are added and locked, do not invent permanent relationships, height differences, recurring jokes, wardrobe, or personality traits for them.

## Ensemble continuity

- Preserve each character's own anatomy and reference hierarchy.
- Do not blend eyes, hands, fur, feathers, proportions, or accessories between characters.
- Define height and staging relationships only after both master models are available.
- In multi-character prompts, label every input reference by character name and role.
- Assign each line of dialogue unambiguously.
