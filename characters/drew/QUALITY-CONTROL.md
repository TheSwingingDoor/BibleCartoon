# Drew Production Quality Control

An image is approved only when every critical check passes. A strong joke does not excuse character drift.

## Critical identity gates — all must pass

- [ ] Same compact mature flamingo head as the master.
- [ ] Eyes remain small, expressive, avian, and readable with controlled white, iris, pupil, and catchlight.
- [ ] Beak is the same pale-and-dark downturned flamingo beak.
- [ ] Neck is long, slim, and smoothly S-curved.
- [ ] Build remains average and healthy.
- [ ] Arms are layered wing-arms, not human arms or extra wings.
- [ ] Each visible wing-hand uses three feather-digits.
- [ ] Nail tips are tiny, pale, and avian—not human nails or talons.
- [ ] Legs and webbed feet remain flamingo-like.
- [ ] Solid black bow tie is present and correctly placed.
- [ ] No traits from another character have migrated into Drew.

Failure of any critical gate means **reject**.

## Performance checks

- [ ] Expression is selected from the approved system.
- [ ] Gaze lands on a specific subject or object.
- [ ] Smile, if present, uses beak seam, eyelids, and posture—no teeth or lips.
- [ ] Wing gesture is readable and anatomically feathered.
- [ ] Pose suits Drew's composed, economical acting style.
- [ ] Emotion is strong enough to read but not exaggerated into a new face.

## Scene-variable checks

- [ ] Clothing is explicitly requested or narratively necessary.
- [ ] Clothing fits over the canonical body and preserves wing anatomy.
- [ ] Props are requested, necessary, and correctly gripped.
- [ ] Martini, when present, contains exactly three olives on one pick.
- [ ] Temporary scene elements have not been treated as permanent anatomy.
- [ ] Other characters use their own identity references.

## Style checks

- [ ] Entire image is black, white, and restrained gray wash.
- [ ] Linework looks hand-drawn with natural variation.
- [ ] Feather texture follows anatomy rather than filling surfaces randomly.
- [ ] Drew carries the strongest useful contrast.
- [ ] Background is subordinate and does not crowd the joke.
- [ ] No 3D, glossy, anime, photoreal, vector-flat, or color contamination.
- [ ] No watermark, signature, or stray text.

## Caption and composition checks

- [ ] Speaker is unmistakable.
- [ ] Caption or balloon does not cover eyes, beak, bow tie, gesture, or key prop.
- [ ] Text is verbatim and legible.
- [ ] Silhouette reads at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Crop does not amputate essential gesture or species identifiers.

## Scoring

After the critical gates pass, score five areas from 0–20:

| Area | Passing score |
|---|---:|
| Identity fidelity | 19 |
| Anatomy and expression | 18 |
| Scene continuity | 17 |
| Style fidelity | 18 |
| Composition and caption readiness | 17 |

Total approval threshold: **90/100**, with no critical failure.

## Review record

Record the prompt version, reference asset IDs, output filename, score, reviewer, date, and any exceptions. If a useful image fails, keep it outside approved assets and document what must be corrected.
